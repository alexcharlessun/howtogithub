# Making Coffee

This skill teaches you how to brew coffee.

## Prerequisites

- Coffee beans
- Grinder
- Water
- Kettle

## Steps

1. **Grind Beans**: Grind 20g of coffee beans to a medium-coarse consistency.
2. **Boil Water**: Heat water to 93°C.
3. **Brew**: Pour water over the grounds in a circular motion.
4. **Serve**: Enjoy your coffee!
